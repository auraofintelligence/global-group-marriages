United Nations of Love

The family business and love marriage across cultures that Luke Nathan Hayes wants to build.

Concept artwork

The family I want to build

I'd like to build a family with people from different cultures who choose to share love, care and enterprise. We'd work out what we want to make together and what each person wants to pursue in their own life.

This is my proposal within the wider Global Group Marriages framework. Other people can form their own groups, starting with a throuple and growing to whatever size works for them. This kind of marriage will suit some people and add one more possibility to the many ways families already live.

We choose each other

I want to be free to say who and what brings me joy and helps me live responsibly and abundantly. Everyone involved gets to define that for themselves.

My romantic relationships

I want to choose the women who also choose me. Each person has the same freedom to choose their own partners.

How the group grows

I encourage women in the group to vet and invite men they'd like to join. I'd have the same say as any other partner. The people taking on each shared commitment decide together, on equal terms.

What we'd take care of together

Family decisions

Hear from everyone affected, including children in ways that suit their age.

Everyday care

Make time for rest, meals, health and the people who need support.

Family enterprise

Agree on pay, ownership, risk and what each person wants to keep independent.

Culture and hospitality

Make room for each other's languages, food, stories and occasions.

Future generations

Think about the care, education and relationships that children can count on over time.

Making things right

Listen when something hurts, take responsibility and revisit agreements when life changes.

A cultural contribution to world peace

The name draws on the idea of coming together across borders. Our contribution would come through Joyful Responsible Abundance in daily life. We'd share food and hospitality, make music and stories, build businesses and keep turning up for people across cultures.

I'd like us to see how close we can come to unconditional love by caring about each other through change. Each person would keep the freedom to set boundaries, choose their relationships and decide which commitments they want to make.
