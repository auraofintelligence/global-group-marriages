## Visual editorial rules

Each hero and editorial scene on this site must be distinct and made for the content around it. Do not reuse one GGM image on another page or card. Luke permits borrowing from his other repositories, with a link to the original, but the resulting use within this site should still be unique. Keep the site visual when replacing an unsuitable picture.

The United Nations of Love is Luke's personal dream. Use his supplied real photographs or clearly labelled AI concepts grounded in his likeness where the page is about him; never call an unrelated generic man Luke or present a generated family as a real relationship. The real portrait in `assets/images/un-of-love-luke-portrait.webp` is the identity anchor. Distinguish documentary photos from imagined scenes in alt text and captions.

Show group marriage rather than defaulting to a couple. Include both shared daily life and joyful ceremonies shaped by different cultures, without treating any one ceremony as representative of a whole culture. Luke's wardrobe may be purple, black, vibrant, casual, formal, Celtic, beachwear, eclectic or local clothing suitable to a particular event; his hair may be down, up or covered by a hat. Some imagined scenes may include pregnant adults where the context calls for it. Check the actual pixels and their place in the page before publishing.
