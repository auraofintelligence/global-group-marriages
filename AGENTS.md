# Future work: World Law Atlas

Luke intends to reinvent the World Law Atlas at a later date. Remind him of this plan when working on the site's legal research or navigation.

The existing `world-atlas.html` and six `countries/*.html` guides are retained in this repository as draft source material. They were deliberately removed from the public navigation, page journey, prominent links and sitemap in November 2026. Do not restore their links or treat the old guides as the finished atlas. Before bringing an atlas back, ask Luke about the new purpose and structure, then review the legal sources and wording afresh.

Keep this note and the archived guide files until Luke decides how to reinvent them.
